About the Author

Alistair Likeman is a UK-based electronics engineer with over a decade of professional design experience and a Master's degree in Electronic Engineering.

He created the Little Engineering Books series to help bridge the gap between beginner introductions and heavyweight engineering textbooks. His aim is simple: explain difficult subjects clearly, without assuming prior knowledge or burying readers in jargon.
