Sensors to Code

Measure

Explore how temperature, light, force and position become electrical signals, and learn how to measure them.

Explore Op-Amps

Convert

Learn how sampling and ADCs turn real-world signals into numbers, and how DACs turn digital values back into analog signals.

Explore Converters

Send
