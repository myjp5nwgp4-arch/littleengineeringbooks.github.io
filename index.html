About the Author

Alistair Likeman is an electronic design engineer with 15 years of experience, a UWE Bristol graduate based in the UK. After countless hours explaining circuits to colleagues, interns and students, he realised that most electronics books miss the mark—they're either too simple or way too complex.

These books are his attempt to fix that. They're written in plain English, aimed at anyone who wants to understand how real circuits work without drowning in theory.
