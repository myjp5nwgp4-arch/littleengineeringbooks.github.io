Why These Books Exist

Too many electronics textbooks are written like dissertations. Thirty pages to explain an op-amp buffer. Equations without intuition. Jargon without context.

Little Engineering Books are different. We explain what circuits do, how they work, and where you'd use them—all in plain English. No theory overload. No hand-waving. Just clear, practical explanations that stick.
