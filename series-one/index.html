Series One

Sensors to Code

Follow a measurement from the physical world to useful digital information. A sensor produces an electrical signal; supporting circuits can buffer, amplify or filter it before an ADC samples it into data. A microcontroller can then process that data and exchange it with peripherals or other systems. Start with the topic you need, or follow the series in order.
