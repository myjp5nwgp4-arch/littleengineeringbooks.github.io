Op-Amps

Learn how operational amplifiers turn weak, noisy signals into useful circuit behaviour. This book explains the most common op-amp circuits without drowning you in jargon.

Back to Series Next: Converters

What you’ll learn

  • What an op-amp actually does and why it matters.
  • How gain, feedback and input/output limits affect performance.
  • How to build common amp, comparator and filter circuits.
  • When op-amps are helpful—and when a different circuit is better.

Key topics

  • Non-inverting and inverting amplifiers
  • Voltage followers
  • Comparators and thresholds
  • Integrators and differentiators
  • Filters
  • Real-world op-amp limitations
