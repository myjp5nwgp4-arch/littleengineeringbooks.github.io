Analog and Digital Converters

Every digital system needs a way to sense and generate real-world signals. This book explains how ADCs and DACs work, why they matter, and how to select the right one.

Back to Series Next: Digital Communications

What you’ll learn

  • How ADCs and DACs bridge the analogue and digital worlds.
  • What sampling, resolution, and speed really mean.
  • How flash, SAR and successive approximation converters differ.
  • When to use a DAC and how to make sense of converter performance.

Key topics

  • Sampling and quantisation
  • ADC architecture choices
  • DAC types and use cases
  • Key conversion errors
  • Choosing the right converter
