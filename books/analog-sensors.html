About the Author

Alistair Likeman is an electronic design engineer with 15 years of experience, based in the UK. He has spent years helping engineers and students make sense of difficult topics in electronics, and much of that work shaped the style of Little Engineering Books.

He believes most engineering explanations are too academic, too long, or too vague. He wanted to write books that are practical, readable, and small enough to actually finish.

Why These Books Exist

After many years in electronics design, Alistair noticed the same pattern again and again: people wanted practical understanding, not endless theory. They wanted to know what a circuit does, why it matters, and when to use it.

Little Engineering Books was created to fill that gap. The aim is simple: explain real engineering topics clearly, and keep the focus on what matters in the real world.

Each book is written to stand alone, but they also fit together as a broader system-level story—from sensing the physical world, to conditioning signals, to converting them, and then communicating them efficiently.
