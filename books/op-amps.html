Analog Sensors

Understand how real-world quantities become measurable electrical signals. This book covers the sensors and circuits you need to start reading the physical world with confidence.

Back to Series Next: Op-Amps

What you’ll learn

  • How pressure, temperature, light, force and position become analogue voltages and currents.
  • How bridge circuits and dividers work in the real world.
  • How to interpret sensor outputs without getting lost in theory.
  • How to choose and troubleshoot common sensing front ends.

Key topics

  • Voltage dividers
  • Thermistors and RTDs
  • LDRs and light sensors
  • Strain gauges and bridges
  • Current shunts and instrumentation
  • LVDTs and RVDTs
